How This Works

One deck, one morning, fifteen minutes

The schedule mirrors the Drive folders. A section with seven decks runs for seven mornings; a section with three runs for three. Every session uses the same four beats, so agents learn the rhythm once and the content can change underneath it.

The four beats that make up every 15-minute session
Minutes Beat What happens
0–2 Hook One real number or one real call moment. No agenda, no recap of yesterday.
2–7 Teach One idea only. If a deck needs two ideas, it needs two mornings.
7–12 Drill Everybody says something out loud. Pairs, round-robin, or a live listen.
12–15 Commit One behaviour for today and one number to watch. Then straight onto the phones.

The Schedule

Click or tab to any block to open the full lesson plan. The left edge of each block shows whether the deck exists yet, so the chart doubles as a build tracker. Scroll the chart sideways to reach the later weeks — the section column on the left stays put. Blocks carry a short label; the full title is on the section cards below and in every lesson plan.

Back to back runs the decks with no gaps and finishes soonest, but a section can start mid-week. Always on a Monday is tidier to talk about and adds about three weeks of empty mornings. Changing the start date snaps to the nearest Monday. Any session landing on a UK bank holiday is flagged in red.

    Editing This Page

    Every lesson plan lives in one file, assets/curriculum-data.js. Nothing in the markup needs touching to change the curriculum.

    • Add or remove a deck: add or remove an entry in that section’s modules array. The chart lengthens or shortens and every later date shifts automatically.
    • Mark a deck as built: change its status to ready, and bump decksReady on the section.
    • Add the Drive link: set driveUrl on the section and the folder name becomes a link.
    • Reorder sections: move a section object inside PROGRAMME.sections. Numbering and dates follow position.
    • Rename a module to match your deck: edit title, and label for the short version shown on the chart. Keep label words to eight characters or fewer so they fit a day column.

    Deep links work per session, so #s1m1 opens the first deck of section one. Useful for pasting a single lesson plan into Slack the night before.
